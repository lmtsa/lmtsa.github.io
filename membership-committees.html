Committees

Chapter Fundraising Committee

Do you want to get more involved in the fundraising efforts of LM-TSA? If so, join the committee to plan fundraisers for the chapter and American Cancer Society. Members work with officers and advisors, brainstorm ideas, attend meetings, create promotional materials, and serve as fundraising leaders.

Outreach Committee

This new committee runs community STEM programs including FIRST Lego League Jr., Merion Robotics, and STEM Night. Due to the quarantine restrictions, the Outreach Committee is planning on transitioning much of its activities online to continue supporting young student learning.
